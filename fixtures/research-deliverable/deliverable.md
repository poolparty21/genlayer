# Market Research Deliverable: AI Coding Agents — Q3 2026

## Deliverable summary

This document summarizes the competitive landscape of AI coding agents as of
Q3 2026, focusing on (a) adoption trends, (b) pricing models, and (c) the
emerging "verification layer" category. The claims below are each supported by
the evidence files in `evidence/`.

## 1. Adoption trends

- AI coding agents are used by a majority of professional developers for
  code-generation tasks: **61%** of surveyed developers reported using an AI
  coding agent at least weekly (evidence: `evidence/adoption-survey.md`).
- Enterprise adoption is driven by pull-request review and test-generation
  workflows rather than full autonomy (evidence: `evidence/enterprise.md`).

## 2. Pricing models

- Subscription pricing dominates: median seat price is **$20/month** for
  individual plans across the top five tools (evidence: `evidence/pricing.md`).
- Usage-based pricing (per task or per token) is emerging among newer entrants
  targeting autonomous agents (evidence: `evidence/pricing.md`).

## 3. The verification layer

- A new category is emerging: services that verify whether an AI agent's
  submitted work satisfies the original task agreement. Key requirements
  identified: deterministic checks, independent adjudication, and auditable
  PASS/FAIL decisions (evidence: `evidence/verification-layer.md`).

## Conclusion

The market is moving from "code completion" to "autonomous agents", which
creates a structural need for independent verification — the category
AgentzProof addresses.
