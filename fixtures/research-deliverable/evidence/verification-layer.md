# Verification layer (source: agent-tooling landscape analysis, 2026)

- Emerging category: independent verification of agent-produced work.
- Common requirements: (1) deterministic checks on deliverables, (2)
  independent or decentralized adjudication, (3) auditable PASS/FAIL records,
  (4) evidence provenance.
- No dominant incumbent as of Q3 2026 — category is wide open.
